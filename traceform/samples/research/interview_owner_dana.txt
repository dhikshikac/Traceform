Interview: Dana Okafor, owner of Second Rise Bakery (Ithaca, NY)
Date: Oct 14, 2026 · 38 minutes · in person, back of the shop
Interviewer: Harry

H: Tell me a bit about how the bakery started.
Dana: I started selling at the farmers market in 2019, just sourdough and a couple of cardamom buns. The shop on Cayuga Street opened in 2022. It's still mostly me and two bakers. We start at 4 a.m.

H: Who comes in?
Dana: Weekday mornings it's nurses from the hospital and grad students. Saturdays it's families and people who've been following us since the market. A lot of them text me to ask if there's any rye left. Honestly the texting is out of control. I'll be shaping loaves and my phone is going off.

H: Why do you want a website now?
Dana: Pre-orders. People want to reserve a loaf so they don't drive over and find the shelf empty. We sell out of the country loaf by 10:30 most Saturdays. Right now pre-orders are a Google Form and a paper list taped by the oven, and I've double-booked loaves more than once.

H: What should the site feel like?
Dana: Like the shop. Flour on the counter, the chalkboard, the radio playing. Not a fancy French patisserie. We're not that. Every bakery website I look at has a croissant on a marble slab and a script font. I don't want that. I'd rather show the actual bread, even the ugly ones.

H: Anything you'd never want on it?
Dana: Please no "artisanal" or "crafted with love." Everybody says that. And no wheat-stalk logo. My nephew made me one once and I never used it.

H: What do customers ask you most?
Dana: "What time do you sell out?" and "Is this one vegan?" The cardamom buns aren't, they have butter. The country loaf and the rye are. People with allergies ask about nuts a lot because we do a walnut loaf on Fridays.

H: How do you decide what to bake?
Dana: The menu changes with the season. In the fall it's the apple rye and pumpkin seed loaf. I post the week's bakes on Instagram every Monday, and that post always gets the most saves of anything I do.

H: How do people pay?
Dana: In the shop it's card or cash. For pre-orders I'd rather they pay at pickup. A lot of my regulars are older and don't want to put a card into a website.

H: Anything else?
Dana: I want people to know who bakes the bread. Marcus and Lena have been with me since the market. They should be on the site, not just me.
