# Customer intercepts, Second Rise Bakery

Oct 18 (Saturday) and Oct 21 (Tuesday), outside the shop. 6 customers, 5 to 10 minutes each. Notes typed up the same day.

## C1, nurse, comes on weekdays before shift
- Comes in around 6:45 a.m. on her way to the hospital.
- "I just need to know it's open and that there's something left."
- Has never pre-ordered; did not know the Google Form existed.

## C2, grad student, weekly
- Found the bakery through Instagram.
- "The Monday post is how I plan my week, honestly."
- Would pre-order on her phone if it took under a minute.

## C3, retired teacher, Saturdays with grandkids
- Has been coming since the farmers market.
- "I don't want to make an account for bread."
- Prefers to pay in the shop. Would leave a phone number but not a card.

## C4, father of two, Saturdays
- Showed up at 11 last Saturday and the country loaf was gone.
- "If I could hold one loaf until noon, I'd never go anywhere else."
- Child has a tree-nut allergy; always asks which bakes have walnuts.

## C5, new to Ithaca
- Walked past and came in because of the chalkboard sign.
- "It smells amazing in here, the website should show the inside."
- Looked up the bakery online first and only found the Instagram.

## C6, regular, vegan
- "I always have to ask which ones are vegan, it's a bit awkward."
- Would like the labels on the shelf and online to match.
